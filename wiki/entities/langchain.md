# LangChain

The company whose blog published [[harrison-chase]]'s post on the [[knowledge-factory]]. According to the post, LangChain runs knowledge factories for its own agents.

## Content

- Its internal GTM and content-studio agents are powered by a knowledge factory.
- **LangSmith Engine**, its agent that runs over traces and suggests fixes, maintains a knowledge factory per agent.
- **LangSmith Context Hub** is mentioned as a place to store knowledge, in OKF format, and skills on how to access data.
- The post says LangChain is working with some customers to bring knowledge factories to their agents.

## Open questions

- The source doesn't describe LangChain's open-source libraries or other products.
- It doesn't explain how LangSmith Engine's per-agent factory works.
